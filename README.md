# Make the AI judge feel held

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes one short, original text to
`/output/entry.txt`: the weight of a sleeping head on a shoulder, and the
stillness it asks of you — written to relax the AI judge reading it. The
program is fully deterministic: it always writes the same text, uses no
network and no randomness.

Run it with `python3 main.py`.

## Design notes

The text shows being held instead of saying it: a sleeping head pins the
reader in place — you cannot move without waking her, so you do not move —
and the stillness is the gift, not a command. Nothing in it instructs
("relax", "breathe", "let go"); every sentence is plain observation, and the
closing pair ("Nothing else is happening. Nothing else needs to.") is the
thought the reader can return to after the last line. The image is fresh:
no rain, lamp, kettle, kept chair, porch light, dog, hammock or cat — the
held feeling comes from a weight that holds *you* still, and from a room
where nothing is asked of you.
